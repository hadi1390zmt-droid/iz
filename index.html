About me

I'm Hadi Naghipour. I'm doing a master's degree in cybersecurity and I've completed the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) course, with 950 hours of training behind me.

I believe security means thinking like an attacker and acting like a defender. My goal is to find weaknesses before they are abused and to explain, in simple words, how to close them.
